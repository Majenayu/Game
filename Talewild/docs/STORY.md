# Talewild: Story outline

Tone: adventurous, warm, funny, with real emotion. Safe for age 7 and up, no gore. Short dialogue lines, cutscenes
under 90 seconds and skippable, a story beat or reward every 3 to 5 minutes. All text goes into locale tables.

Names that break the "no reuse" rule are already replaced: **Nib** (was Pip; Pip is a character in Orb Arena),
**Tobin** (was Bram; Bram is a character in Sunfetch), **the Pale Ink** (was the Quiet Ink; "the Quiet" is Sunfetch's
antagonist). The owner also chose: world **Inkmere**, Motif **Blaze** (was Ember), **Keepsakes** (were Charms) and
island **Forgepeak** (was Cinderpeak). See DECISIONS.md.

---

## Premise

Inkmere is a living story. Every island grew from an old legend, and every legend lives on as Echoes.
Lately, the sky turns pale for a moment at dusk and whole pages of the world go blank: a signpost loses its words, a
song loses its tune, an Echo fades. People call it **the Pale Ink**.

The Scribe's grandmother **Elowen**, the most famous Chronicler of the islands, vanished the night the sky first went
pale. She left an empty study, an unfinished map and a sleepy ink-drop Echo called **Nib**.

**Goal:** win the **Eight Pages** from the Keepers, reach the Blank Isle, find Elowen and rewrite the ending.

## The truth (revealed over the game)

The Pale Ink is the story's own ending, slowly writing "The End" across the world. Years ago Elowen found the last page
and saw what would happen if nothing changed: the whole book would close at once. To buy time she wrote a rule:
*"Some pages must be closed with care, so the story can go on."* She and her partner **Maren Vale** founded the
Redactors to close fading pages gently. Then Elowen hid in the margins of the world to search for a way to write new
pages instead.

Maren lost her child **Ivo**, whose favourite legend faded first. Grieving, she took the rule much further: if every
legend is erased, no one can ever lose anything again. She now leads the Redactors to blank the whole world.

**Foreshadowing from Chapter 2:** the Redactor seal on an erasure order uses Elowen's handwriting.

## Endings

| Ending | Requirement | What happens |
| ------ | ----------- | ------------ |
| **Close the Book** (bittersweet) | Default | The Scribe and Elowen close the old book to stop the Pale Ink. A new, smaller world begins. Many legends are lost; Nib stays. Maren walks into the blank, alone |
| **Rewrite** | 25 or more Tales completed | The Scribe rewrites the last page and pushes the Pale Ink back. The world is restored. Maren refuses help and leaves; the Redactors break apart |
| **Share the Pen** (best) | All 8 Pages and 40 Tales | Scribes and Redactors share the pen: stories end when they should, and new ones begin. Maren, with Amity's help, writes Ivo's legend again; it becomes the first new Echo in years (a Sketchling). Elowen comes home |

Each ending has a final cutscene (under 90 seconds) and a credits scroll over the Scribe's journal, filled with the
Echoes the player bound.

## Tales (format)

One Tale per Echo family (70 in the full game, 22 in Episode 1), 1 to 3 minutes each:

1. **Hook:** the Echo behaves strangely in the overworld (for example Kettlekin keeps whistling at an empty house).
2. **Small task:** a short walk, a puzzle or a gentle battle.
3. **Choice:** one of two endings to the Tale; each opens a different Legend Path option.
4. **Reward:** the family's signature Verse and a Bond boost.

Example, **Kettlekin, "The Last Guest":** Kettlekin whistles at the house of an old baker who moved away. The Scribe
finds her at the harbour, worried nobody visits anymore. Choice: bring her back for tea (Kettlekin learns *Warm
Welcome*, a healing Verse) or bring her tea at the harbour (learns *Steam Whistle*, a Tempo boost).

## Side content plan

**Episode 1 side quests (15):**

1. The Signpost Without Words (Quill Harbor): help rewrite the town's signs.
2. Lighthouse Oil: fetch oil from three fishermen before dusk.
3. The Crab Census: count Saltsnips on the beach (a counting mini-game).
4. Lost Kite: climb the sea cliffs to free a child's kite from Kitefins.
5. A Recipe for Courage: cook soup for a nervous apprentice (cooking mini-game).
6. Pebble Wall: rebuild the harbour wall with Pebblepups.
7. Cairn Road: follow Cairnback's stones to a hidden meadow.
8. Morning Song: wake a Morrowlark that overslept for a week.
9. The Fishing Contest (fishing mini-game).
10. Juno's Missing Corner: find the lost corner of a map.
11. Glowcap Delivery: carry glowcaps through the dark without dropping one.
12. Murkit's Second Tail: find out why a Murkit's shadow follows a stranger.
13. Puzzle Box of Odric: open a silent hermit's box (puzzle-box mini-game).
14. Night Market: help a merchant who only trades after dark.
15. The Umbrella Thief: Brollybats keep stealing umbrellas. Why?

**Full game:** 40 side quests, 120 lore pages, 20 secret places, 15 mini-games: fishing, kite race, cooking, rhythm
rite, puzzle boxes, a card-like story game ("Fableboard"), boat race, lantern sorting, glider rings, sand drawing,
choir game, book stacking, star mapping, storm timing, memory replay.
