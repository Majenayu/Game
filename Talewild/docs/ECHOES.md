# Echo families (Phase 0 list)

70 families, 180 Echoes:

| Group | Families | Forms | Echoes |
| ----- | -------- | ----- | ------ |
| Episode 1 (islands 1 and 2) | 22 | 16 with 3 forms, 6 with 2 forms | 60 |
| Chapters 3 to 8 (6 per island) | 36 | 3 forms each | 108 |
| Legendary | 12 | 1 form each | 12 |
| **Total** | **70** | | **180** |

Each brief gives the **silhouette rule** (what must stay recognisable in every form), the **shape language** and the
**signature feature**, plus the legend the Echo embodies. Form names are written in Phase 4 with the art.

Names are original. They were spot-checked against well-known creature games; a full web check of all 70 names is
scheduled before art production (Phase 4). One early idea, "Pebblepaw", was dropped because it matches a naming style
from the Warriors book series.

Temperament decides the Binding Rite: **Shy** rhythm, **Bold** hold-and-release, **Playful** follow-the-pattern,
**Wary** choose the offering.
