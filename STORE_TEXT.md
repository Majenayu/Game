# Store text: Orb Arena

## Title

Orb Arena

## Short description (<= 160 characters)

Fast multiplayer arena. Steer a glowing orb, dodge shots and win 90 second rounds with the most kills. Bots fill empty seats.

## Long description

Orb Arena is a quick online shooter for up to 8 players. You are a glowing orb in a neon arena. Move, aim and
shoot. Three hits eliminate an orb, and it respawns after two seconds.

Each round lasts 90 seconds and the player with the most kills wins. After a short scoreboard the next round
starts in the same room, so you can keep playing with the same people.

There is never an empty lobby. Bots fill free seats and step aside when real players join.
Grab the glowing pickup for 5 seconds of rapid fire.

Play on desktop or mobile. No sign-up needed.

## Controls

Desktop

- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Mouse: aim
- Left click (hold): shoot
- Esc: leave to menu

Mobile

- Left thumb stick: move
- Right thumb stick: aim and fire
