# Store text: Orb Arena

## Title

Orb Arena

## Short description (<= 160 characters)

Online orb arena shooter. Grab 1000+ powerups, use skills, unlock characters and win modes with friends. Bots fill empty seats.

## Long description

Orb Arena is a fast online shooter for up to 8 players. You are a glowing orb in a neon arena. Move, aim, shoot and use skills to win every round.

Play four modes: Deathmatch, Team Battle, Coin Rush and King of the Hill. Quick Play changes the mode and the arena every round, so every match feels new. Six arenas include walls, mazes and lava.

Grab powerups from the arena. There are more than 1,000 unique powerups, from rapid fire and triple shot to ricochet, frost and vampire. Pick two skills such as Dash, Bubble, Pulse, Mine, Blink, Burst or Repair, and choose from seven characters with different strengths.

Earn coins and XP in every match. Unlock characters, skills, colors, trails and bullet styles in the shop. Collect your daily reward, finish daily and weekly quests, spin the lucky draw wheel and complete the 12 level campaign against bots, ending with a boss fight.

There is never an empty lobby: bots fill free seats and step aside when real players join. Invite a friend with one click. Play on desktop or mobile with no sign-up.

## Controls

Desktop

- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Mouse: aim
- Left click (hold): shoot
- Space and E: skills
- Esc: back to menu

Mobile (landscape)

- Left thumb stick: move
- Right thumb stick: aim and shoot
- On-screen buttons: skills
