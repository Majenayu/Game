## Title

Elementick

## Short description (under 160 characters)

Tag-team stick fighting with 10 elements and 30 powers. Pick your element, build a squad of two, and beat the masked champion Null.

## Description

Elementick is a fast 1v1 stick-figure fighting game. Pick one of 10 elements, team up two fighters, and tag between them mid-fight.

- **10 elements, 30 powers.** Fire, Water, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Earth, Leaf, Light, Gravity and Time. Each beats two and loses to two, so every matchup matters.
- **Tag team.** Swap fighters at any time. Each has their own health and energy.
- **No cooldowns.** Powers cost energy and take skill to land. Time a perfect guard to turn defense into offense.
- **Level up every fighter.** New powers at levels 8 and 20, stronger ranks as you grow, perks to choose at 15, 30 and 45.
- **Champion Road.** 40 champions across 8 districts of Brightvale, then the masked fighter Null, who has never been hit.
- **Ranked Ladder** against AI rivals, **Zombie Survival** with boss waves, **Training**, and **Local 2P** on one keyboard.
- **Make your fighter yours** in the Avatar Forge: heads, faces, hats, scarves, capes, auras and win poses. Looks never change stats.

No pay-to-win. Everything is earned by playing.

## Controls

Keyboard:
- Move: A / D or arrow keys. Jump: W, Up or Space. Dash: Shift or double tap.
- Attack: J or Z. Heavy: K or X. Guard: L or C.
- Powers: U, I, O or 1, 2, 3. Tag swap: Q or Tab. Pause: Esc or P.
- Keys can be changed in Settings.

Gamepad: A attack, X heavy, B dash, Y tag, LT guard, LB / RB / RT powers.

Touch: drag on the left side to move, buttons on the right.

Local 2P (one keyboard): Player 1 uses W A S D, F G H, R T Y, Q, Left Shift. Player 2 uses the arrow keys, M , ., J K L, /, Right Shift (or the numpad).
