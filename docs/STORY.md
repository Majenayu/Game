# Sunfetch: Story Script (Phase 0 draft, English)

Tone: warm, full of wonder, a little funny. Safe for age 7 and up. No gore: shadows "dissolve" into drifting motes.
Delivery: skippable comic panels made from layered vector art with slow parallax and a typewriter text effect. No voice.
Every panel stays under 25 words. All lines will move into a locale table (`story.*` keys) once the English is final.

Names follow the owner's decisions in DECISIONS.md (title Sunfetch, hero Tindle, antagonist The Quiet).

---

## Cast

| Character | Who they are |
| --------- | ------------ |
| **Tindle** | The starter Spirit. A small round candle flame who has never seen the sun. Curious about everything, scared of almost nothing, and terrible at sitting still. |
| **Sparrow** | A bird-shaped flame who left the nest early. Talks in short bursts, flies before thinking, always comes back to help. |
| **Clinker** | A slow coal golem who used to warm the bakery ovens of the Meadow. Gentle, strong, quietly proud of his crust. |
| **Prism** | A crystal sprite from the Glass Caverns. Thinks in three directions at once and finishes other people's sentences, usually wrong. |
| **Moth** | A ghost moth who was once afraid of light. Now she follows it everywhere and feels a little guilty about her snacks. |
| **Flare** | A young star that fell when the Beacon broke. Hums all the time, and the world seems to hum back. |
| **Old Lamp** | The mentor. A dented brass lamp who kept the Great Beacon for a thousand years. Gentle, funny, forgets where he put his glasses (he has no eyes). |
| **Cinder** | A fierce rival spirit. She believes the last sun should belong to whoever is strong enough to carry it. Underneath, she is afraid of being small. |
| **Tolliver** | A shy moth shopkeeper who whispers prices and apologises to his customers. |
| **The Quiet** | A quiet, endless darkness. Not evil. Lonely. It has never seen its own shape, and it thinks the light will finally show it. |

## Prologue (5 panels, about 20 seconds)

1. Once, the Sun lived in the Great Beacon, and every night was short and kind.
2. But the Sun grew tired. Its light thinned like the end of an old candle.
3. One quiet night, the Beacon cracked. Five shards of light fell across the world.
4. The dark spread. Shadows crept out to drink whatever light was left.
5. In a forgotten lighthouse, an old lamp flickered. "Wake up, little one. We have work to do."

*(Tindle blinks awake. The first room starts directly after this panel.)*

## World 1: Hollow Meadow

**Intro**

1. Old Lamp: "The Meadow was the first place the sun touched each morning. Now listen. Hear that? Nothing."
2. Old Lamp: "Your light is your life, little one. Stay bright, and the shadows will keep their distance."
3. Tindle looks at the dark grass. A hundred red eyes look back. Tindle waves.

**Boss: Thornback**

- Intro: A hill of brambles uncurls. A shard of light glows deep inside its thorns.
- Thornback: "Mine. Warm. MINE."
- Outro: The brambles loosen. A small hedgehog shadow sneezes out the shard and rolls away, embarrassed.

**Outro**

1. The first shard floats into Tindle's flame. For a moment, the Meadow turns green.
2. Old Lamp: "One down. Four to go. And I think somebody was watching us."
3. On a far hill, a red flame turns away. Cinder: "Not bad. For a candle."

## World 2: Glass Caverns

**Intro**

1. Deep caves full of crystal. Every crystal remembers the light it once held.
2. Old Lamp: "Careful. Down here, your light bounces. So do the things that hunt it."
3. Prism (if unlocked) or a crystal echo: "Three ways in. Two ways out. One way that is a wall."

**Boss: Mirror Queen**

- Intro: A queen of glass steps out of every wall at once.
- Mirror Queen: "Which of us is real, little light? Look closely. The real one always casts a shadow."
- Outro: The copies shatter like ice on a pond. The real Queen bows and returns the shard. "You saw me. Thank you."

**Outro**

1. Two shards now. Tindle glows a little wider.
2. Cinder waits at the cave mouth, arms crossed. "Give me the shards. I can carry them faster."
3. Tindle shakes her flame no. Cinder smiles like a storm. "Then we race."

---

## World 3: Drowned Bell

**Intro**

1. A city under the sea. Its great bell still rings, slow and sad, every minute.
2. Old Lamp: "Bubbles are safe. Currents are not. And please do not lick the jellyfish."
3. Tindle floats down through the dark water, like a lantern lowered into a well.

**Boss: The Tide Choir**

- Intro: Three shadow singers rise around the bell. Their song pushes the sea in rings.
- The Tide Choir: "Sing with us. Sing forever. Sing until your light goes quiet."
- Outro: The song breaks into laughter. The singers drift up as bubbles, carrying the shard to Tindle.

**Outro**

1. Three shards. The bell rings once, bright and happy, for the first time in years.
2. Old Lamp, quietly: "Cinder went ahead to the Orchard. She is burning everything to find the next shard."
3. Tindle looks up at the far red glow on the horizon.

---

## World 4: Ash Orchard

**Intro**

1. An orchard of burnt trees. The air tastes like toast left too long.
2. Old Lamp: "Too wide a light and the fire spreads. Sometimes being small is being smart."
3. Cinder's voice echoes through the smoke: "This shard is mine. Turn back, candle."

**Boss: Cinder's Crown**

- Intro: Cinder wears a crown of flame made from the stolen light of the Orchard.
- Cinder: "Everyone keeps telling me I am too much. Let's see if you are enough."
- Phase 2 line: "Why are you still glowing? Why don't you ever go out?"
- Outro: Cinder's crown flickers out. She sits in the ash, small and tired. "I just didn't want to disappear."

**Outro**

1. Tindle sits beside Cinder. Neither of them says anything for a while.
2. Cinder hands over the shard. "The last one is at the Spire. The Quiet has it. Be careful."
3. Old Lamp: "Four shards. One left. And now, I think, the hardest part."

---

## World 5: The Silent Spire

**Intro**

1. A tower made of silence. No colour. No sound. Even Tindle's flame looks grey here.
2. Old Lamp: "The Quiet lives at the top. It took the last shard. I do not know why."
3. As Tindle climbs, colour returns to every step she lights. The tower notices.

**Boss: The Quiet (3 phases)**

- Intro: The top of the tower is not a room. It is the whole night sky, and it is looking at Tindle.
- The Quiet, phase 1: "...light. So close. Come closer."
- The Quiet, phase 2: "Everything runs from me. Everything goes out near me. Why do you stay?"
- The Quiet, phase 3: "Show me. Please. I have never seen what I look like."

**The finale choice**

Before phase 3, Cinder appears at the top of the stairs.

- Cinder: "Give me the shards. I can end this now. Or... let me stand with you."
- Choice A, **"Stand with me."** Cinder joins as an ally in phase 3 and in the endings.
- Choice B, **"I'll do this alone."** Cinder leaves. "Fine. Shine by yourself." She returns only in the credits.

Phase 3 is a light puzzle. The Quiet stops attacking and asks to be lit. The player chooses by what they do:

- **Keep attacking until it fades** → Ending 1, Reignite.
- **Stop attacking, stand still beside it and let your light fill it** (a hold-to-shine meter) → Ending 2, Share the Light.
- **With at least 10 of the 12 Souls rescued**, a third option appears: the Souls gather around Tindle. **Walk to the edge of the
  tower** → Ending 3, Walk Away.

## Endings

### Ending 1: Reignite (win by force, bittersweet)

1. The Quiet thins like morning mist and drifts apart.
2. The five shards rise into the Great Beacon. The sun bursts back to life.
3. The world wakes up warm. Rescued Souls wave from their homes.
4. Old Lamp looks at the sky. "It's bright. It's beautiful. And it's very quiet up there now."
5. At night, the sky is empty. No stars. Tindle watches it for a long time.

*Epilogue line:* "The sun came home. But something was missing from the night."

### Ending 2: Share the Light (merciful, best)

1. Tindle shines into the Quiet. The dark fills with tiny points of light.
2. The Quiet sees its shape for the first time. It is a night sky, full of stars.
3. The Quiet, softly: "Oh. I'm... beautiful?" Tindle nods so hard her flame wobbles.
4. Together they relight the Beacon. Day comes back, and night comes back too, kind and bright.
5. Rescued Souls gather on the lighthouse steps. (If Cinder is an ally, she sits beside Tindle, pretending not to smile.)

*Epilogue line:* "Now the sun keeps the day, and the Quiet keeps the stars. Both of them wave at sunset."

### Ending 3: Walk Away (secret, 10 or more of the 12 Souls)

1. Every rescued Soul stands around Tindle at the edge of the tower.
2. Mira: "We don't need one big sun. We could be a lot of little ones."
3. One by one, the Souls float up and become new stars. The Quiet gasps.
4. The world learns to live by many small lights: lanterns, windows, candles, friends.
5. Tindle and the Quiet sit on the edge of the Spire and watch the first ever sunrise made of everyone.

*Epilogue line:* "The Beacon stayed dark. Nobody minded. There was light in every window."

## Spirit bios (for the Spirit select screen)

- **Tindle:** "Small, round and very curious. Tindle has never seen the sun, but she's sure she'll like it."
- **Sparrow:** "Flies first, thinks later, apologises never. Sparrow's light points wherever she's going."
- **Clinker:** "Used to warm the bakery ovens. Slow to start, impossible to stop, smells faintly of toast."
- **Prism:** "Sees every problem three ways and solves at least one of them. Steer the shards by moving."
- **Moth:** "Once afraid of light, now she can't stay away. Slips through small shadows and nibbles their glow."
- **Flare:** "A baby star that fell with the Beacon. Hums all the time. Hit on the beat and the world hums back."
