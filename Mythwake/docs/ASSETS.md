# Third-party assets

Every third-party asset is recorded here **before** it is used. If a license is unclear, the asset is not used.

Allowed: CC0 (Kenney, ambientCG, Poly Haven, Freesound CC0, OpenGameArt CC0), CC-BY (with credit, OpenGameArt),
SIL OFL fonts (Fontsource, Google Fonts), original work.
Never: CC-BY-SA, GPL, non-commercial (NC), ripped or unlicensed assets, AI tools whose terms forbid commercial use, or
the look, names or move lists of any existing creature game.
